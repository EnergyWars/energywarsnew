## 2. Summary

Energy Wars is an offline game. The app does **not collect, store or transmit any personal data** to the developer or to third parties. It contains **no ads, no in-app purchases, no third-party analytics, tracking or crash reporting services** and requires **no user account**. The app does not request the internet permission and does not open any network connections.

## 3. Data on your device

So that you can continue playing, the app stores the following data exclusively in its private storage on your device:

- **Progress:** conquered regions, stars, records, completed challenges, purchased research, position on the world map, total enemies defeated, best combo and monsters already discovered.
- **Settings:** language, sound and vibration.

This data contains no information about you as a person, is never sent off your device by the app and cannot be viewed by the developer. You can delete your progress at any time in the game via "Reset progress". Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data.

## 4. Permissions

The app only uses the **vibration** permission (`android.permission.VIBRATE`) for haptic feedback in the game. Vibration can be turned off in the game. No other permissions are requested – in particular no access to the internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## 5. Android backup

The app allows Android backup (Auto Backup). If you have turned on backup in your device settings, **Android** may include your progress and settings in your personal device backup with Google and restore them on a new device. This transfer is performed by the operating system into your Google account; the developer has no access to it. Since Android 9, such backups are end-to-end encrypted when a screen lock is set. Google is responsible for the backup according to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can turn off backup in your Android settings at any time.
