## Titel
Energy Wars: Tower Defense

## Kurzbeschreibung
Beam solar power to your towers, survive the night and conquer enemy bases.

## Vollständige Beschreibung
Harvest the sun. Survive the night. Burn their bases.

Energy Wars is a tower defense and conquest game where energy is everything. Your soldier parachutes onto an unknown, fog-covered map. Find a safe spot, place your core and build a power grid: solar panels, water wheels and fire pits generate power, while distributors and storage send it to your towers through visible energy beams.

PLAN, THEN FIGHT
• Plan without time pressure: build, connect, relocate and upgrade – with undo and reset.
• Then start the day: waves of enemies attack, and the night brings much stronger monsters.
• At dawn the night monsters burst into flames – if your core holds out until then.

A GRID THAT MATTERS
• Every beam has a range and a loss – short paths save energy.
• Storage gets you through the night, lamps keep the darkness away.
• Status mode shows at a glance where energy is missing and why.

TOWERS, WALLS AND MORE
• Zapper, laser tower, Tesla coil, plasma mortar, shield generator, medic tower, mobile tower and more.
• Bulwarks form a protective wall that repairs itself with energy.
• Target priorities, focus attacks and many upgrade paths for every building.

EXPLORE AND CONQUER
• A world map with several regions, each with its own randomly generated landscape.
• Fog of war: your soldier scouts, cuts trees and clears rocks.
• Around 30 enemy types from bug nests and field camps – from quick crawlers to huge bosses.
• Bug nests cover the land in slime – destroy them to build again.

LONG-TERM GOALS
• Campaign: conquer region after region.
• Survival: last 5, 10 or 15 days – or play endless mode for a new record.
• Challenges award skill points for a research tree with new buildings and bonuses.
• Creative mode: all buildings, no fog, unlimited money – perfect for experimenting.
• Checkpoint: after a defeat, retry the last day.

FAIR AND OFFLINE
• Buy once, play everything: no ads, no in-app purchases.
• Plays completely offline, no account and no internet connection needed.
• No data collection, no trackers.
• English and German, portrait mode, for phones and tablets.

All graphics and sounds are generated procedurally in the game.
